# Product principles

Life Hub is not a productivity tracker.

It is a digital world shaped by the user's real life.

The Companion notices, but does not judge.

Most of the time, it simply lives alongside the user.

Real-world actions should leave visible traces in the Companion, room, wardrobe, collectibles and memories.

Never optimize the user.

Make real life feel a little more worth living.

---

你认真活过的每一天，都不该白白消失。Life Hub is a second layer of real life: the game is there to make life
feel like something, never the other way round. These principles outrank the details of any feature.

## The loop

Real activity → Life Hub state → HAKU reacts → 能量罐 → unlocks, items, changes to the world → widgets and
Lock Screen → Mike sees HAKU again. People open the app to see what HAKU is up to, not to log anything.

## What we don't build

- No productivity judge: no scores, ratings or penalties. Rest and fun are part of life too.
- No "do task, tick box, get XP".
- AI is not the product and there is no chatbot home screen. AI powers the world; it isn't the world.
- Models never decide game rules. Cans, unlock conditions and the state machine are code.

## How often HAKU speaks

| Level | How often | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lives alongside | Most of the time | Mike chills, HAKU chills; Mike sleeps, HAKU sleeps |
| Notices | Now and then | "你今天好像有点没电。" An observation, never a verdict |
| Nudges | Rarely | "出去走十分钟？" Rare, so it carries weight |

Before HAKU says anything, ask whether it needs to speak at all.

## Visible traces

Real life leaves something you can see, not just a number: short-term room traces (a bandage, the monitor
on, sunlight), long-term changes to HAKU and the room. Keepsakes come only from real life and are worth far
more than shop items. Humor and empathy, never punishment.

## Privacy

Raw life data, state and HAKU's memory stay on the iPhone. They leave the device only through a service the
user connects on purpose.

## Now: one user, 2 to 4 weeks

- No generalizing or platform rewrite. The four modes are Mike's, not a template.
- After the current batch (places, 副业, gym invite, work states, morning states, 能量罐, room traces), no
  new features for 2 to 4 weeks. Only work that makes HAKU feel more alive (idle moments, line rotation,
  reaction timing) and bugs found on the device.
- The question that matters: did Life Hub ever change something Mike did in real life? The app records
  these "change moments" by itself, and the Sunday memory card mentions them in HAKU's voice, positives only.
- Whether HAKU feels alive matters more than ten new features.
